Biggest problems

1.

Meta description still missing

Google writes its own snippet when you don't provide one, and it's usually bad. This directly hurts click-through rates from search results.

Recommendation:

"SpinLGX delivers logistics intelligence through AI-powered contract optimization, freight audits, and supply chain consulting. Save millions on transportation costs."

2.

Title tag is too short

Current: "SpinLGX" (7 chars). Doesn't include keywords or value prop.

Try:

"SpinLGX | Logistics Contract Optimization & Freight Audit Services"

(62 chars, includes primary keywords)

3.

No Open Graph tags

When shared on LinkedIn, Facebook, or Twitter, the site won't display properly—no preview image, no description. Makes it look unprofessional.

4.

AI-generated content (likely)

Detected buzzwords: seamless (2×), leverage, unlock, streamline, optimize. These are telltale signs of AI-written copy that hasn't been humanized.

Replace with specific outcomes: "Clients saved $2.4M in 2025" instead of "seamless optimization."

5.

No forms on homepage

No clear conversion path. Visitors can't contact you or request a quote without hunting around. Add a contact form or prominent CTA button to a contact/quote page.
